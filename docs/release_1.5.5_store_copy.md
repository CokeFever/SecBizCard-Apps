### en
Secure, instant, professional — SecBizCard is the privacy-first way to exchange and manage business cards.

■ Privacy First
Your data stays on your device. We never store personal information on our servers, ensuring absolute data sovereignty. What's yours stays yours.

■ Instant Exchange
Share your business card with a simple QR code scan. Professional and instant exchange without manual data entry.

■ Secure Connection
Encrypted end-to-end communication. Only you and your intended contact can access the exchanged information. Temporary session data is automatically deleted after 5 minutes.

■ AI-Powered Card Scanner
Scan physical business cards with your camera. Our AI recognition (Google Cloud Vision) extracts contact details in multiple languages, including English, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean. If a scan isn't perfect, you can optionally report it to help us improve — always your choice.

■ Backup & Restore
Back up your saved contacts to your personal Google Drive and restore them seamlessly on a new device. Backups are encrypted and stored in your own Google account — we never have access.

■ Google Contacts
Directly save selected contacts to Google Contacts, keeping your professional network synced across all your devices.

More features are coming soon.

## Apple — App Review Notes (paste into App Review Information → Notes)

This release adds an OPTIONAL "report bad recognition" feature for our business-card OCR.

How it works & privacy:
- It is strictly opt-in and confirmed on every submission. Nothing is sent unless the user explicitly agrees on that specific card.
- When the user consents, we upload the card photo plus the recognized text and result, used ONLY to reproduce and improve OCR accuracy. Not used for tracking or advertising, not sold.
- Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, stored in the user's own uid-scoped path, and deleted within 30 days.
- Full consent screen shown in-app (all 5 languages); details in the Privacy Policy: https://ixo.app/privacy
- Account & data deletion: https://ixo.app/privacy#delete-account

App Privacy answers were updated accordingly (Contact Info, User Content / Photos, User ID — all "App Functionality", linked, NOT used for tracking).

To review the feature: scan a card, then on the review screen tap Back; if the result looks poor you'll be offered the optional feedback prompt.
